7-Day Elite Workout Plan

Follow this expert-designed routine to transform your physique.

Monday - Back

Builds a strong 'V' taper and improves posture.

  • Pull-ups: 4 Sets x Max Reps
  • Lat Pulldowns: 3 Sets x 12 Reps
  • Deadlifts: 3 Sets x 8 Reps
Tuesday - Legs

Increases lower body power and metabolism.

  • Squats: 4 Sets x 10 Reps
  • Lunges: 3 Sets x 12 Reps
  • Leg Press: 3 Sets x 15 Reps
Wednesday - Shoulders

Gives you broad, capped shoulders.

  • Shoulder Press: 4 Sets x 10 Reps
  • Lateral Raises: 3 Sets x 15 Reps
Thursday - Arms

Focuses on bicep peak and tricep thickness.

  • Bicep Curls: 4 Sets x 12 Reps
  • Tricep Dips: 4 Sets x 12 Reps
Friday - Abs

Strengthens core and reveals 6-pack.

  • Plank: 3 Sets x 1 Minute
  • Crunches: 4 Sets x 20 Reps
  • Leg Raises: 3 Sets x 15 Reps
Saturday - Cardio

Burns fat and improves heart health.

  • Running: 20 Minutes
  • Jump Rope: 10 Minutes
Sunday - Rest

Recovery is Key

Muscle grows while you sleep. Take this day to stretch and recover.
