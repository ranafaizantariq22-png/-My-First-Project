The Hall of Fame

Learn from the legends who redefined the limits of the human body.

Arnold Schwarzenegger

"The Austrian Oak"

7-time Mr. Olympia winner. He transformed bodybuilding into a global sport and became the greatest icon in fitness history.

Achievement: 7x Mr. Olympia

Ronnie Coleman

"The King"

Known for his incredible strength and size. He holds the record for most Mr. Olympia wins (8) along with Lee Haney.

Achievement: 8x Mr. Olympia

Jay Cutler

"The Quadfather"

Famous for his massive quads and intense rivalry with Ronnie Coleman. He won the Olympia 4 times.

Achievement: 4x Mr. Olympia

Chris Bumstead (CBum)

Classic Physique King

The modern face of bodybuilding. Known for his incredible vacuum pose and classic aesthetics.

Achievement: 6x Classic Olympia

Dorian Yates

"The Shadow"

Introduced "High Intensity Training" (HIT). His grainy muscle density changed the sport forever.

Achievement: 6x Mr. Olympia

Phil Heath

"The Gift"

Famous for his perfect muscle genetics and 3D look. He dominated the stage for 7 consecutive years.

Achievement: 7x Mr. Olympia

Frank Zane

"The Chemist"

The king of aesthetics. He proved that symmetry and proportion can beat massive size.

Achievement: 3x Mr. Olympia

Tom Platz

"The Golden Eagle"

Legendary for his leg development. No one in history had better squats or leg intensity than him.

Achievement: Best Legs in History
